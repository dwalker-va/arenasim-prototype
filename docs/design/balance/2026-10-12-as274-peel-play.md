# AS-274: the Peel play — first measurement

Tier: **directional**. Success condition, stated before the sweep: `--expect
improvement` — giving a team the Peel play raises its win rate against the same
team without it.

**Result: the mechanism works, the value claim is not shown.** With the play a
team answers about seven times as many ally-in-danger windows with crowd
control on the attacker inside one global cooldown. The win rate does not move:
**+0.1pt (z=0.57) over 14,400 paired matches, a run that resolves 0.3pt.** The
play changed the peeling side's result in 4.2% of matches, and those changes
split almost evenly.

## What was built

`situation::Play::Peel`, gated under `AiProfile::TeamPlan`. While
`ally_in_danger` names a teammate (dying and pressured), every other unit on
the team puts a peel step at the head of its chain: its peel tools
(`class_ai::peel::peel_tools`), in its own order, on the teammate's
`danger_attackers` (the enemies pressuring it, nearest first).

| class | peel step |
|---|---|
| Mage | Frost Nova with a melee or pet attacker in its reach, else Polymorph (not on its own target) |
| Warlock | Death Coil, else Fear (not on its own target) |
| Paladin | Hammer of Justice |
| Hunter | Concussive Shot on a melee or pet attacker, else a Frost Trap thrown onto it |
| Rogue | Kidney Shot |
| Druid | Cyclone (not a pet, not its own target), else Entangling Roots on a melee or pet |
| Priest | Psychic Scream with an attacker inside it |
| Shaman | Frost Shock on a melee or pet attacker |
| Warrior | none in the kit (no Hamstring or Intimidating Shout) |

Every cast goes through the cast gate (range, sight, cooldown, resource, the
hold rule). An attacker the team already holds is not peeled, and a movement
peel answers only a melee or pet that is not already rooted or slowed. A peel
is traced: `situation.play` names the play and the ally, and
`situation.play_step == "peel"` marks a choice the peel step made.

The Druid's Roots on a pet chasing a dying teammate (AS-245's request) is this
play's Druid step. AS-245 asked for it before the teammate is dying, too; that
earlier trigger is still AS-245's.

## Tier 4: the probe (`tests/peel_play_probe.rs`)

Six 2v2 comps with peel tools on both sides, both maps, seeds 1–16, all
`TeamPlan`: 192 matches per arm. The windows are the trace's `ally_in_danger`
onsets.

| arm | windows | answered within one GCD | mean survival (10s cap) | reached the cap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Peel | 750 | 76 (10.1%) | 7.58s | 52.7% |
| withheld | 776 | 12 (1.5%) | 7.40s | 52.3% |

The answered rate is up (z=7.2). Survival is +0.18s, which the bank cannot
resolve (z=1.1). The probe asserts the first as a significant rise (z ≥ 3) and
the second as no significant harm (z > −2), both in units of the bank's own
noise.

**Why only 10% of windows are answered.** A per-window breakdown of the first
bank (164 windows) put most of them out of reach of the play as built:

- **68 had no teammate decision in the GCD.** The teammate was mid-cast, or
  crowd-controlled, or dead. A play that reorders a chain only acts when the
  chain is asked.
- **61 had a decision under the play but no peel.** The tool was on cooldown
  (Frost Shock, Kidney Shot, Hammer of Justice, Psychic Scream), out of range
  (Polymorph past 30yd, Kidney Shot outside melee), or the attacker was the
  peeler's own target (no Polymorph).
- **12 had a teammate that was not under the play:** every attacker was
  already held, or the teammate was a Warrior.

## Tier 5: the head-to-head

Every 2v2 team of two distinct classes, without double healers (30 teams, all
with a peel tool), against every other team: 900 cells. Both maps, seeds 1–4,
both teams under `TeamPlan`, in three arms per seed:

- **NN:** the play withheld from both teams;
- **PN:** team 1 peels;
- **NP:** team 2 peels.

Each PN match is paired with its NN twin and scored for team 1, and each NP
match with its twin and scored for team 2. Every pair asks whether giving this
side the play changed its result. No team-1 rate is pooled across the two
assignments.

**Control: 7,200/7,200 NN rows identical** in winner and duration to main's
`TeamPlan` at the same seeds (6,862 decisive, 3,146 distinct durations). So
the branch changes nothing under `TeamPlan` but the play itself.

| slice | pairs | peeling side wins: without → with | delta | flips (+/−) | z | floor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BasicArena, team 1 peels | 3,600 | 49.3% → 49.2% | −0.1pt | 77/80 | −0.16 | 0.7pt |
| BasicArena, team 2 peels | 3,600 | 50.1% → 50.5% | +0.4pt | 91/76 | +1.08 | 0.7pt |
| PillaredArena, team 1 peels | 3,600 | 45.2% → 45.3% | +0.1pt | 66/63 | +0.18 | 0.6pt |
| PillaredArena, team 2 peels | 3,600 | 45.4% → 45.4% | +0.0pt | 70/70 | 0.00 | 0.7pt |
| **overall** | **14,400** | **47.5% → 47.6%** | **+0.1pt** | **304/289** | **+0.57** | **0.3pt** |

Verdict, by `--expect improvement`: **not an improvement at this size.** The
run rules out an effect above 0.3pt either way, and says nothing below it.

**Per peeling-team class:** 13 slices tested in all (the 4 above and 9 below),
so read the significant ones with that count beside them. The class rows
overlap, because each pair counts once for each class on the peeling team.

| peeling team has | pairs | delta | flips (+/−) | z |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Warlock | 3,840 | +1.2pt | 145/99 | **+2.88** |
| Warrior | 3,840 | +1.0pt | 133/94 | **+2.52** |
| Priest | 2,400 | +0.3pt | 40/33 | +0.70 |
| Rogue | 3,840 | +0.1pt | 22/20 | +0.15 |
| Paladin | 2,400 | 0.0pt | 7/7 | 0.00 |
| Mage | 3,840 | −0.1pt | 97/102 | −0.28 |
| Druid | 2,400 | −0.2pt | 74/78 | −0.24 |
| Shaman | 2,400 | −0.8pt | 49/69 | −1.75 |
| Hunter | 3,840 | −0.9pt | 41/76 | **−3.14** |

The direction is plausible:

- **The Warlock's peel** is Death Coil, a horror that never breaks, and it
  heals the Warlock.
- **The Warrior's row** is a team whose partner peels for it: the Warrior has
  no tool, but it is the teammate most often in danger.
- **The Hunter's peel** spends Concussive Shot and the shared trap cooldown on
  a snare, taking them from its own kite and its Freezing Trap.
- **The Shaman's** spends Frost Shock's cooldown, and the global cooldown ahead
  of its emergency heal.

This is directional evidence for a per-class follow-up, not a finding.

## What this says about the architecture (§7)

§7 asks to watch for plays that fight each other, and whether the wins sit in
discrete plays at all. This measurement says:

- **The play fires.** The tier-4 probe resolves it, and the trace names every
  peel.
- **It barely changes outcomes.** 4.2% of matches moved, and they moved both
  ways. The kit is usually on cooldown, out of range or mid-cast when a
  teammate is in danger, so a reordering has little to reorder.
- **Where it does change outcomes, the sign depends on the class.** That is
  the "continuous trade-off" §7 names: a Hunter's Concussive Shot spent on a
  peel is not spent on its own kite. The plays here are not fighting each
  other; each one is fighting the class's existing uses of the same cooldowns.

Not tuned toward a win, per the card.

## Graduate or delete (§4.5)

At the 0.8 milestone sweep, Peel **graduates** (made unconditional) only if
all of these hold:

1. **The head-to-head improves the win rate.** Run this design (PN and NP each
   against NN, scored from the peeling side, both maps) on the milestone's
   cells. The overall delta must be positive and significant (z ≥ 1.96), with
   no map × assignment slice significantly negative.
2. **No class's peel step is significantly negative.** If one is, that step is
   removed from `peel_tools` before the play graduates.
3. **The probe still passes** (`peel_play_probe`).

Otherwise it is **deleted**: the `Peel` variant, the peel steps,
`class_ai::peel`, `WithheldPlays` and the config fields. On today's numbers it
would be deleted. The one way out is to narrow it to the classes whose
peel helps, which is a new card measured the same way.

## Reproduce

Arms are one binary: the play is withheld per team by config, so there is no
patch to apply.

```bash
cargo build --release
S=docs/design/balance/sweeps
python3 $S/2026-10-12-as274-gen.py h2h 4 > /tmp/h2h.jsonl   # = 2026-10-12-as274-h2h.jsonl.gz
target/release/arenasim --batch /tmp/h2h.jsonl --out /tmp/h2h.csv --jobs 4 --trace-mode off
python3 $S/2026-10-12-as274-oriented.py /tmp/h2h.csv --expect improvement
# the control: main's TeamPlan on the NN rows (strip the withheld fields first)
python3 $S/2026-10-12-as274-oriented.py --control nn-branch.csv $S/2026-10-12-as274-nn-main.csv.gz
cargo test --release --test peel_play_probe -- --ignored --nocapture
```

Everything above recomputes from the committed CSVs except the probe table,
which needs the probe run (about a minute). The Legacy byte-identity batch
(`gen.py legacy 2`: the same 900 cells, both maps, 3,600 matches) is
`2026-10-12-as274-legacy.csv.gz`. It matches main's output byte for byte, with
3,577 kills and 2,000 distinct durations.
