# AS-285: no hard CC on a unit the team already holds

Tier: **directional**. Success condition: **no regression** (`--expect nothing`).
This is a correctness fix, measured to show it costs nothing, not to buy win rate.

## The change

Every hard-CC chooser asks one predicate, `CombatContext::held_in_hard_cc`,
and the cast gate asks it again of every targeted cast whose aura holds
(`cast_guard::applies_hold`). A unit is **held** when it carries an aura of the
`holds` set (Stun, Fear, Polymorph, Incapacitate, Cyclone; the set
`situation::locks_healer` now delegates to) with more than `CC_CHAIN_WINDOW`
(0.5s) left. Two refinements:

- **Chain window.** Inside the last 0.5s a new CC chains onto the hold. The
  Rogue's Kidney Shot onto its own expiring Cheap Shot fires exactly there.
- **A breaking hold.** A hold that breaks on accumulated damage (Fear, Psychic
  Scream) does not count against a CC that never breaks (a stun, a horror, a
  Cyclone) while the team is attacking its bearer. The team's damage is about
  to break the Fear, so the stun becomes the lock that lasts. A Polymorph or a
  Freezing Trap still counts, because the team holds its damage for those.

Root is not a hold: a rooted unit still casts and swings. The Warlock's and
Mage's own CC lists counted it, and the Druid's Cyclone asked `is_ccd`, which
counts it too. Those sites now fear, sheep and cyclone a rooted unit. The Death
Coil peel still passes over a rooted melee, because a root already peels it.

A chooser that drives movement does not skip a held target. The Paladin's
Hammer of Justice dip, the Priest's scream dip and the Hunter's trap dip and
kite setup still walk to a held healer. Only the cast waits.

## Attribution

`sweeps/2026-10-12-as285-attribute.py` replays each of the 1,692 moved matches
on both binaries with the trace on. It finds the first decision that differs
and classifies it from the two traces:

| first divergent decision | matches |
|---|---|
| REFUSED: a hard CC cast in the base arm onto a held target, refused (`TargetAlreadyCCd`) or passed over by its chooser | 471 (Kidney Shot 306, Hammer of Justice 90, Freezing Trap 52, Fear 23) |
| ALLOWED: a CC the base arm refused, cast inside the chain window or on a Fear the team was breaking | 932 (Cyclone 348, Fear 235, Polymorph 198, Death Coil 147, Kidney Shot 4) |
| ALLOWED: a CC the base arm refused because of a root | 204 (Fear 195, Polymorph 9) |
| OTHER: first divergence not a CC choice | 85 |

The Cyclone row cannot tell a root from the chain window: the base arm's
Cyclone chooser traced neither.

Every moved match traces to a decision the change makes. The refusals are the
card's fix, and the larger count of newly allowed CC comes from using one
predicate. Neither direction moves the outcome at this tier.

An earlier draft let the breaking-hold exception apply to every CC. The
Warlock then re-feared targets the team was breaking, its own Fears included,
and the CC-pair side lost 3pt (152 / 349 discordant, p<0.001). An incoming CC
that breaks the same way gains nothing, so the exception now applies only to
one that lasts. With held targets also filtered out of the dip and kite-setup
choosers, the pair side still lost on PillaredArena (23 / 53 discordant,
p=0.001). The first divergences were in movement: the Hunter stopped setting up
a trap on a healer that would stay held for only a few more seconds. Those
choosers now keep their targets and only the cast waits.
