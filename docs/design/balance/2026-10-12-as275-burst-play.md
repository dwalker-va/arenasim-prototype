# AS-275: the Burst play — first measurement

Tier: **directional**. Success condition, stated before the sweep: `--expect
improvement` — giving a team the Burst play raises its win rate against the
same team without it, scored from the bursting side, and no DPS class's burst
measures negative (the 2026-10-12 ruling: a play that measures negative in a
context is not used there).

**Result: an improvement, and no class measures negative.** +1.0pt for the
bursting side over 14,400 paired matches (z=7.96, a run that resolves 0.3pt),
significant on every map × assignment slice. Every DPS class's slice is
positive or flat. `BURST_EXCEPTIONS` ships empty.

## What was built

**The situation.** `kill_window` (`situation.rs`): an enemy below
`kill_hp_threshold` (0.35; held until it is back above `kill_hp_release`,
0.45) that no living enemy healer can heal — each one locked (stun, fear,
polymorph, incapacitate, cyclone), out of mana (below `kill_oom_mana`, 20,
the cheapest heal in the book), or out of reach (beyond `heal_range` or out of
sight). A team with no living healer heals nothing. The window names the most
injured such enemy; its `until` is when the first healer that is only locked
comes free, and it has none when no healer's return is known. Thresholds are
`movement.ron`'s `shared:` block. Traced: `situation.kill_window` on every
decision, and `situation_change` events named `kill_window`.

**The play.** `Play::Burst`, gated under `TeamPlan`. While a kill window is
open, every DPS puts its burst step at the head of its chain (below a peel
step), on the window's subject — whoever its own target is:

| class | burst step |
|---|---|
| Warrior | Mortal Strike; then, on its own target, Heroic Strike with none of the 50 rage the chain holds back for the next Mortal Strike |
| Rogue | Sinister Strike, ahead of the Kidney Shot planner's holds and pooling |
| Mage | Frostbolt |
| Warlock | Death Coil (damage and a horror that never breaks), then Shadow Bolt |
| Hunter | Aimed Shot when no enemy is within 20yd, else Arcane Shot |

**The fit.** Under the play, the cast gate refuses any cast on the subject
that would land after the window's `until` — the burst step's and the chain's
alike (`cast_guard::fits_window`, traced `OutlastsWindow { lands_at, until }`).
A cast lands after its cast time (slowed by Curse of Tongues), its flight,
and, for a cast whose only damage is over time (Rend, Corruption, a curse,
Serpent Sting), its first tick. This is the Warrior's Rend-timing awareness:
a Rend whose first tick would land after the lock is not applied.

**The retired rules.** The Warrior's and Hunter's lock-window reorders
(AS-270: 0 of 12,393 and 0 of 21,192 decisions changed) no longer run for a
unit that runs the Burst play. They are kept for `Legacy` — and for a team
with the play withheld, or a class it names as an exception — as
`CombatContext::lock_window`, because deleting them is NOT byte-identical:
22 of the 3,600 `Legacy` matches below changed, every one with a
Warrior+Rogue team. AS-270's "changed no decisions" held for its bank, not
for every match.

**The Priest's Mana Burn window** is a healer rule, outside a DPS play. It is
filed as its own card (see the PR's follow-ups), not changed here.

## Tier 4: the probe (`tests/burst_play_probe.rs`)

Six 2v2 comps (five with a healer each side, one with none), both maps,
seeds 1–48, all `TeamPlan`: 1,152 matches per arm. The windows are the
trace's `kill_window` events. A burst LANDS inside a window when a burst tool
chosen on its subject arrives (cast time, flight, a DoT's first tick) by the
window's `until`, or by its end when no `until` is known.

| arm | windows (with an `until`) | answered by a landed burst | within one GCD | kills secured inside | DPS decisions inside | chose a burst on the subject | of those, landed inside |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Burst | 1,266 (144) | 489 (38.6%) | 285 (22.5%) | 923 (72.9%) | 1,294 | 1,049 (81.1%) | 731 (69.7%) |
| withheld | 1,273 (144) | 480 (37.7%) | 247 (19.4%) | 920 (72.3%) | 1,301 | 935 (71.9%) | 697 (74.5%) |

- **The card's "answered" measure does not move** (z=0.48), and neither do
  the kills secured inside a window (z=0.36). In both arms the DPS's ordinary
  chain already lands the same tools on the same low enemy in about 38% of
  windows, and about 72% of windows end in that enemy's death either way.
  Most windows are short: 1,294 DPS decisions across 1,266 windows.
- **What the play changes is how a window's global cooldowns are spent.**
  81.1% of the DPS's decisions inside a window go to a burst on its subject,
  against 71.9% without the play (z=5.52). The probe asserts this as the
  tier-4 response rate (z ≥ 3), and holds the landed responses, the answered
  windows and the kills secured to no harm (z > −2 each).
- **A smaller share of those bursts land inside the window** (69.7% against
  74.5%, z=−2.40 as a share of bursts). In absolute terms more land (731
  against 697 of about 1,300 decisions, z=+1.49). The extra bursts are
  started late in windows that close first: a window with no `until` ends
  when its enemy dies or its healer recovers, and a Frostbolt still in the
  air then counts as not landed. The fit cannot help there; it has no
  deadline to fit to.
- **The fit almost never fires in natural play**: 4 casts refused over the
  bank. Only 144 of 1,266 windows (11%) have an `until`. The scene tests and
  the staged match are where it is pinned.

The decision z's pool decisions across windows, which are clustered, so they
are optimistic.

## Tier 5: the head-to-head

Every 2v2 team of two distinct classes, without double healers (30 teams),
against every other: 900 cells. Both maps, seeds 1–4, both teams under
`TeamPlan`, every class bursting (`burst_exceptions: []`, so a class the play
could be gated off for is measured too), in three arms per seed:

- **NN:** the play withheld from both teams;
- **BN:** team 1 bursts;
- **NB:** team 2 bursts.

Each BN match is paired with its NN twin and scored for team 1, and each NB
match with its twin and scored for team 2. No team-1 rate is pooled across
the two assignments.

**Control: 7,200/7,200 NN rows identical** in winner and duration to main's
`TeamPlan` at the same seeds (6,844 decisive, 3,214 distinct durations). So
the branch changes nothing under `TeamPlan` but the play itself.
**`Legacy`: 3,600/3,600 rows byte-identical** to main (3,580 kills, 2,014
distinct durations).

| slice | pairs | bursting side wins: without → with | delta | flips (+/−) | z | floor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BasicArena, team 1 bursts | 3,600 | 48.7% → 49.6% | +1.0pt | 62/27 | +3.60 | 0.5pt |
| BasicArena, team 2 bursts | 3,600 | 50.6% → 51.4% | +0.9pt | 52/21 | +3.51 | 0.5pt |
| PillaredArena, team 1 bursts | 3,600 | 44.8% → 46.0% | +1.1pt | 67/26 | +4.15 | 0.6pt |
| PillaredArena, team 2 bursts | 3,600 | 45.6% → 46.8% | +1.2pt | 72/28 | +4.30 | 0.6pt |
| **overall** | **14,400** | **47.4% → 48.5%** | **+1.0pt** | **253/102** | **+7.96** | **0.3pt** |

The play changed the bursting side's result in 2.5% of matches, and those
changes ran 5:2 in its favour.

**Per DPS class.** Two cuts. "Has X" counts every pair whose bursting team
fields X, so a two-DPS team counts once for each and the rows overlap. "X is
the only DPS" takes the DPS + healer teams, where nothing else bursts: clean
attribution, on half the pairs. Ten class slices plus the five above: read the
significant ones with that count beside them.

| bursting team | pairs | delta | flips (+/−) | z |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| has Warlock | 3,840 | +1.7pt | 124/57 | +4.91 |
| has Hunter | 3,840 | +1.5pt | 81/25 | +5.34 |
| has Mage | 3,840 | +1.2pt | 76/31 | +4.25 |
| has Warrior | 3,840 | +1.0pt | 67/29 | +3.78 |
| has Rogue | 3,840 | +0.9pt | 50/14 | +4.38 |
| Warlock the only DPS | 1,920 | +1.5pt | 57/29 | +2.91 |
| Mage the only DPS | 1,920 | +1.0pt | 29/10 | +2.88 |
| Warrior the only DPS | 1,920 | +0.5pt | 13/3 | +2.25 |
| Hunter the only DPS | 1,920 | +0.2pt | 4/0 | +1.50 |
| Rogue the only DPS | 1,920 | −0.1pt | 5/6 | −0.00 |

No slice is negative beyond noise, so no class is an exception. The Rogue and
the Hunter alone beside a healer gain nothing measurable: their burst mostly
reorders a chain that already spends on the same target. They gain in the
two-DPS teams (+0.9pt, +1.5pt), where a partner's lock opens more windows.

## What this says about the architecture (§7)

- **The play fires and the fit works.** The scene tests, the decision grid
  and the staged match pin both; the staged match shows a Hunter swapping an
  Aimed Shot that would land after a 2s stun for an Arcane Shot that lands
  inside it.
- **Most kill windows have no known end.** In the probe's bank only about
  one in nine has an `until` (a locked healer); the rest are healers out of
  mana or out of reach, or no healer at all, where the fit has nothing to
  refuse. In that bank it refused 4 casts. The fit is correct and cheap, and
  rarely the thing that matters; the reordering is what moves results.
- **The tier-4 measure the card named does not see the effect.** The share of
  windows answered by a landed burst is flat while the win rate rises 1pt.
  A play that reorders a chain already aimed at the right enemy changes how
  many GCDs go to it, not whether one does; the probe measures that instead.
- **Unlike Peel, it does not fight the class's existing uses.** Burst spends
  the class's own damage on the enemy it would mostly be hitting anyway, a
  little sooner. Peel spent cooldowns (Concussive Shot, the trap) that the
  class needed elsewhere, and its Hunter and Shaman slices went negative.

## Graduate or delete (§4.5)

At the 0.8 milestone sweep, Burst **graduates** (made unconditional, with the
lock-window rule deleted beside it) only if all of these hold:

1. **The head-to-head improves the win rate.** Run this design
   (`2026-10-12-as275-gen.py h2h`, both maps) on the milestone's cells with
   the exceptions lifted. The overall delta must be positive and significant
   (z ≥ 1.96), with no map × assignment slice significantly negative.
2. **No DPS class's burst is significantly negative** in its "only DPS"
   slice (z ≤ −1.96). A class that is goes into `BURST_EXCEPTIONS`, with that
   row as its finding, before the play graduates. The same run re-checks
   every class already there: one whose slice is no longer negative leaves.
3. **The probe and the decision grid still pass** (`burst_play_probe`,
   `decision_properties`).

Otherwise it is **deleted**: the `Burst` play, the burst steps,
`class_ai::burst`, the fit, `BURST_EXCEPTIONS` and the config fields. On
today's numbers it would graduate.

## Reproduce

Arms are one binary: the play is withheld per team by config.

```bash
cargo build --release
S=docs/design/balance/sweeps
python3 $S/2026-10-12-as275-gen.py h2h 4 > /tmp/h2h.jsonl   # = 2026-10-12-as275-h2h.jsonl.gz
target/release/arenasim --batch /tmp/h2h.jsonl --out /tmp/h2h.csv --jobs 4 --trace-mode off
python3 $S/2026-10-12-as275-oriented.py /tmp/h2h.csv --expect improvement
# the control: main's TeamPlan on the NN rows (gen.py teamplan, run on main's binary)
python3 $S/2026-10-12-as275-oriented.py --control /tmp/h2h.csv $S/2026-10-12-as275-teamplan-main.csv.gz
cargo test --release --test burst_play_probe -- --ignored --nocapture
```

Every arm ran on main at `380af5f` (AS-281 merged) and the branch rebased onto it. Everything
above recomputes from the committed CSVs except the probe table, which needs
the probe run. The `Legacy` byte-identity batch (`gen.py legacy 2`: the same
900 cells, both maps, 3,600 matches) is `2026-10-12-as275-legacy.csv.gz`.
