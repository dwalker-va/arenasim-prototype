# AS-279: Hammer of Justice asks for sight — findings

**Tier: DIRECTIONAL.** Paladin 2v2 cells (`--affects Paladin`, 275 reachable +
8 control, Legacy), paired at identical seeds, per map. Base `36c4702`, after =
this branch. Nothing here may be cited as the Paladin's standing.

## The change

The rotation Hammer of Justice now chooses among the enemies it REACHES (range,
then sight, `class_ai::cast_reach`) and spends a clearance from `cast_gate` on
its target; the dip's cast goes through `cast_gate` on the dip target. The
dip's arrival check asks the same `cast_reach`, so a dip in range behind a
pillar keeps walking (its `MovementGoal::Entity` walk is already
tangent-steered round obstacles) instead of handing over a cast that cannot
land.

Hammer of Justice casts, counted by an instrumented build of each arm

instrumented arm's CSV is byte-identical to its clean arm's on all three maps):

| map | arm | HoJ cast | through a pillar | rotation / dip |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| BasicArena | before | 1,091 | 0 | 755 / 336 |
| BasicArena | after | 1,091 | 0 | 755 / 336 |
| PillaredArena | before | 1,980 | **1** (dip) | 1,419 / 561 |
| PillaredArena | after | 1,979 | 0 | 1,419 / 560 |
| TwinPillars | before | 2,379 | **246** (227 rotation, 19 dip) | 1,746 / 633 |
| TwinPillars | after | 2,329 | 0 | 1,684 / 645 |

The peel path never fires (it is `TeamPlan`-only). On TwinPillars one HoJ in
ten was cast through a pillar; on Nagrand the pillars are wide enough that a
Paladin inside 10yd of its target almost never has one between them.

## Win rate

`scripts/paired_sweep.py --affects Paladin --tier directional`:

- **BasicArena**: before and after CSVs **byte-identical** over 1,415 matches
  (1,413 kills, 2 caps, 953 distinct durations; 1,091 HoJ casts in each arm,
  every one in sight).
- **PillaredArena**: control 80/80 identical; 1 match moved in duration, none
  in winner. No flips: unresolvable, and nothing to resolve.
- **TwinPillars**: control 80/80 identical; 221 matches moved in winner or
  duration, 37 flipped (team-1 frame). CLEAN −0.6pt (z=1.43), AGAINST −0.5pt
  for team 1 (z=1.77), all ns.

Scored from the Paladin's side (`sweeps/2026-10-12-as279-oriented.py`, exactly
one side fields a Paladin, n=2,500 per map):

| map | Paladin before | after | delta | moved (better / worse) | exact sign p |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| PillaredArena | 66.5% | 66.5% | +0.00pt | 0 / 0 | 1.000 |
| TwinPillars | 62.6% | 62.6% | −0.08pt | 15 / 17 | 0.860 |

**Verdict: the defect is gone (246 → 0 casts through a pillar on TwinPillars,
1 → 0 on PillaredArena), and the Paladin's result does not measurably move:
TwinPillars' 32 moved matches split 15/17, below anything this run resolves.

## Reproduce

Inputs, CSVs and scripts are under `sweeps/` (see its README). The counts need
the two instrumented arms: run the counter script against a checkout of each
arm, build it, run the batch with stderr captured, and
`grep -c 'in_sight=false'`.
