## Verdict

1. **TeamPlan beats Legacy head-to-head**, by more than at 0.7: 2v2
   competitive **+5.5pt** (z=27), 3v3 **+4.8pt** (z=8.3), largest on
   TwinPillars (+10.5 / +11.5). Every class gains in 2v2 competitive play.
2. **Plays:** graduate **Burst** (+0.6pt, z=8.3) and **Chain** (win-neutral,
   lockout up). **Delete TakeCover** (−0.2pt, z=−5.2, negative in both
   formats). **Hunt**: graduate with its three exceptions (+0.1pt, z=2.6;
   lifting them costs −0.6pt, 4 for / 183 against). **Pillar**: keep gated
   (−0.1pt overall, but +0.8 on Nagrand against −0.9 on TwinPillars in 2v2,
   and the reverse in 3v3). **Peel**: a ruling (+0.3pt in 2v2 with block 3 at
   −0.1, −0.4pt in 3v3); recommendation is delete.
3. **AS-389's balance shift is larger than measured at merge** for healers.
   In a uniform TeamPlan world on pillar maps the target focus costs the
   Rogue −6.6, the Shaman −7.2, the Priest −4.6 and the Druid −3.5 (2v2
   competitive), and the double-ranged no-healer 2v2 teams gain +4.6 to
   +9.6. Mage+Warlock is the #2 TeamPlan 2v2 comp on Nagrand (70.2%).
4. **AS-391's −5.1 does not replicate**: on fresh seeds the stance is worth
   **+4.3pt** against Legacy in its own 3v3 bank (z=2.3, every block
   positive) and +0.5 (ns) over a 600-cell 3v3 sample.
5. **Legacy moved since 0.7, and the moves are attributed**: Druid +4.1,
   Priest −2.6, Shaman −2.3, Hunter +1.0 (2v2 competitive). The movement
   stops at AS-249: AS-371 and AS-380 changed 9 of 12,960 Legacy matches.
6. **The Paladin is still the strongest class** (2v2 competitive 59-66% on
   every map and profile), and Warrior+Paladin is the #1 TeamPlan 2v2 comp on
   every map (72-78%).
