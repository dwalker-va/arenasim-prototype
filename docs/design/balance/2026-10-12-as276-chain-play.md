# AS-276: the Chain play — first measurement

Tier: **directional**. Success condition, stated before the sweep: the Chain
play locks the other team for longer — counterplay-free seconds, the
instrument `cc-compounds-not-adds` names, with denied seconds and the longest
lockout beside it — and does not cost the chaining side win rate, scored from
the chaining side; and no class's chain measures negative (the 2026-10-12
ruling: a play that measures negative in a context is not used there).

**Result: the chaining side locks the other team longer, and wins slightly
more. No class measures negative.** Counterplay-free seconds rise a little
(+0.04s a match, z≈1.9, not significant); denied seconds (+0.37s) and the
longest lockout (+0.30s) rise clearly. Win rate +0.4pt over 14,400 paired
matches (z=2.20, a run that resolves 0.3pt). `CHAIN_EXCEPTIONS` ships empty.

## What was built

**The situation.** `lock_ending` (`situation.rs`): of the visible enemies the
team holds in a stun, a fear or horror, a polymorph, an incapacitate or a
cyclone (`locks_healer`'s set), the one whose hold ends soonest, the healer on
a tie. Its `until` is when the longest hold on that enemy runs out.

**The play.** `Play::Chain { subject }`, gated under `TeamPlan`, between Peel
and Burst: while `lock_ending` is within `CHAIN_HORIZON_SECS` (4s) of its end,
a unit with a hand-off puts its chain step at the head of its chain. A healer
does not chain while a teammate of its own is dying. When the step does not
fire, the chain below runs as the fallback play orders it (Burst in a kill
window).

| class | hand-offs (`chain::chain_tools`) |
|---|---|
| Paladin | Hammer of Justice |
| Rogue | Kidney Shot |
| Warlock | Fear, then Death Coil |
| Mage | Polymorph |
| Druid | Cyclone |
| Warrior, Hunter, Priest, Shaman | none — no targeted lock whose landing can be timed |

Freezing Trap (lands when walked on), Psychic Scream (an area around its
caster) and a pet's Boar Charge (the pet's decision) are locks, but none has
a landing the play can time, so none is a hand-off.

**The timing — the inverse of the Burst fit.** A hand-off lands when
`cast_guard::lands_after` says: cast time (Curse of Tongues included) and
flight, the model the Burst fit uses. It is DUE when it lands in the lock's
last `HANDOFF_LEAD` (0.25s): not after `until`, which frees the enemy in
between, and not earlier, which spends the new lock alongside the old one.
Either miss is traced `MissesHandoff { lands_at, until }`. A hand-off not due
yet whose moment is within one global cooldown, and that every other gate
would let go (`pre_cast_ok_but_hold`), HOLDS the unit's global cooldown:
anything cast now would still be on it then. A hold is a chain step with no
choice in the trace.

**The hold rule, at landing.** The cast gate refuses a hard CC on an enemy its
team holds for more than `CC_CHAIN_WINDOW` (0.5s). Under the play a hand-off
onto the lock's subject is judged where it LANDS
(`cast_guard::hold_lands_after`), so a 1.5s Polymorph begun to land in a stun's
last moments is not refused at its start. Everywhere else the offset is 0, so
the rule is unchanged outside the play.

**What a hand-off asks of its subject** (`chain::suits`):

- **Immunity.** Not onto a unit immune to damage or to new auras (Divine
  Shield, a Cyclone lock — nothing lands on a cycloned unit).
- **Diminishing returns.** Not onto a unit immune to the hand-off's category.
  A hand-off that shares a category with the lock still goes: the engine
  replaces a same-category CC when the new one lands, but in the lock's last
  0.25s that costs at most 0.25s against the hand-off's own diminished
  duration (a Polymorph re-cast on its own Polymorph adds 5s). An earlier
  version refused these; it never handed off, because the lock was almost
  always the chaining unit's own.
- **Breakable locks.** A hand-off that deals damage (Death Coil) never lands on
  a lock that breaks on damage (Polymorph, Freezing Trap, Fear).
- **Damage on the subject.** A hand-off that breaks on damage (Polymorph, Fear)
  or shields its subject (Cyclone) is not put on an enemy the team is hitting
  or has a damage over time on.
- **One at a time.** No hand-off while a teammate is mid-cast on one: the
  snapshot carries no cast target, so any teammate casting a chain tool counts.

**Why any enemy, not only the healer.** The architecture's example chains the
locked enemy healer. Built that way first, the play handed off 6 times in the
probe's 768 scored matches. A team rarely holds the healer with a lock its
OTHER member can extend: one member made the lock, usually with the tool that
would have extended it, and the breakable tools cannot go on a healer the team
is hitting. A stun on the kill target, by contrast, is extended by any
teammate's stun. With `lock_ending` over every enemy the probe's bank hands
off 192 times.

## Tier 4: the probe (`tests/cc_lifecycle_probe.rs`)

scored against its NN twin from the CHAINING side: what it inflicted on the
other team, per match, read off every frame. "Locked" is the
`is_incapacitating` set; counterplay-free is two or more of a team locked at

By chaining class (overlapping slices), the longest lockout rises for every

errors, and counterplay-free seconds, healer-locked seconds and wins to no
fall beyond 2. All five hold.

**The cost: held global cooldowns.** The bank holds a global cooldown on
16,034 decision frames, about 267 seconds over 768 matches — 0.35s a match,
four-fifths of it the Rogue's.

## Tier 5: the head-to-head

Every 2v2 team of two distinct classes without double healers (30 teams)
against every other: 900 cells. Both maps, seeds 1–4, both teams under
`TeamPlan` (Peel and Burst on as shipped), every class chaining
(`chain_exceptions: []`), three arms per seed:

- **NN:** the play withheld from both teams;
- **CN:** team 1 chains;
- **NC:** team 2 chains.

Each CN match is paired with its NN twin and scored for team 1, each NC match
for team 2.

**Control: 7,200/7,200 NN rows identical** in winner and duration to main's
`TeamPlan` at the same seeds (6,844 decisive, 3,215 distinct durations).

distinct durations).

Strike, for the moment.

## What this says about the architecture (§7)

- **Time, not value.** The play needed no pricing: "land in the last 0.25s"
  and "hold the global cooldown when the moment is within one" are facts
  about the lock's `until`, as §4.3 proposed.
- **Counterplay-free seconds barely move in 2v2**, and cannot move much: it
  needs two of a team locked at once, one of them its healer, which in a 2v2
  is the whole team. The chain lengthens single lockouts (the longest lockout
  and denied seconds rise clearly); it rarely adds a second simultaneous one.
  A play aimed at counterplay-free seconds would be CROSS-CC — lock the
  second enemy while the healer is locked — which is a different play.
- **Win rate sees it**, faintly: +0.4pt overall, carried by the Warlock and the
  Mage.

## Graduate or delete (§4.5)

At the 0.8 milestone sweep, Chain **graduates** (made unconditional) only if
all of these hold:

1. **The head-to-head does not cost win rate and the lockout rises.** Run this
   design (`2026-10-12-as276-gen.py h2h`, both maps) on the milestone's cells
   with the exceptions lifted. The overall delta must not be significantly
   negative (z > −1.96) and no map × assignment slice significantly negative;
   and `cc_lifecycle_probe`'s assertion test must pass (denied seconds and the
   longest lockout up by 3 paired standard errors, counterplay-free seconds,
   healer-locked seconds and wins not down by 2).
2. **No class's chain is significantly negative** in its "only chainer" slice
   (z ≤ −1.96). A class that is goes into `CHAIN_EXCEPTIONS`, with that row as
   its finding, before the play graduates; the same run re-checks every class
   already there.
3. **The scenes, the staged match and the decision grid still pass**
   (`chain_scenes`, `chain_staged`, `decision_properties`).

Otherwise it is **deleted**: `Play::Chain`, the chain steps, `class_ai::chain`,
`hold_lands_after`, `lock_ending`, `CHAIN_EXCEPTIONS` and the config fields.
On today's numbers it would graduate.

## Reproduce

Arms are one binary: the play is withheld per team by config.

```bash
cargo build --release
S=docs/design/balance/sweeps
python3 $S/2026-10-12-as276-gen.py h2h 4 > /tmp/h2h.jsonl   # = 2026-10-12-as276-h2h.jsonl.gz
target/release/arenasim --batch /tmp/h2h.jsonl --out /tmp/h2h.csv --jobs 6 --trace-mode off
python3 $S/2026-10-12-as276-oriented.py /tmp/h2h.csv --expect improvement
# the control: main's TeamPlan on the NN rows (gen.py teamplan 4, run on main's binary)
python3 $S/2026-10-12-as276-oriented.py --control /tmp/h2h.csv $S/2026-10-12-as276-teamplan-main.csv.gz
cargo test --release --test cc_lifecycle_probe -- --ignored --nocapture
```

Every arm ran on main at `51b5c046` (AS-293 merged) and the branch at `3afc613f` on it.
Everything above recomputes from the committed CSVs except the probe table,
which needs the probe run. The `Legacy` byte-identity batch (`gen.py legacy
2`: the same 900 cells, both maps, 3,600 matches) is
`2026-10-12-as276-legacy.csv.gz`.
