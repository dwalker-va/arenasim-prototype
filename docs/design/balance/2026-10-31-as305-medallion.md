# AS-305: the Medallion's declared price

**Tier: DIRECTIONAL** (a node arm, talents proposal §4 and §6.5 item 4). **Question:** what is
the Medallion's crowd-control break worth in item budget under its AI rule, so its declared
price can stand? **Success condition:** the Medallion, played against an item of the same budget
spent on health, measures level within the run's resolution.

**Answer:** the break is worth about **24 budget points** (24 health). The Medallion ships as
the break at a declared 24 plus 24 health, its Champion trinket budget (48.3). Against 48 health
it measures **-0.12pt (95% +-0.42), McNemar z=1.29**: level.

## The rule being priced

The wearer breaks the first LONG hold that lands while the Medallion is ready: a stun, fear,
polymorph or incapacitate with 2s or more left (`CC_BREAK_MIN_HOLD_REMAINING`). It holds the
Medallion when a teammate is going to dispel every long hold on it (an urgent dispel in scope,
castable now, owed to the wearer), for at most `DISPEL_WAIT_GRACE_SECS` (2s), and breaks once
that stops being true. Roots and slows alone never ask it. Nothing is used from inside a
Cyclone (`CC_BREAK_TAKES_CYCLONE = false`, as in TBC), and the break leaves a silence alone.

## Method

- **Cells.** Every 2v2 on BasicArena with a wearer class X in team 1 slot 0 and a partner P,
  against every distinct enemy pair; no double-healer side. 60 x 30 = 1,800 cells.
- **Seeds.** Three blocks of 4 seeds (305000+, 306000+, 307000+): 7,200 matches a block,
  21,600 an arm. Per-class effects are read in each block separately and pooled.
- **Arms.** One release binary (`46b8fe37`, the shipped rule; arm 0 ran `b3bf0542`, which
  differs only in a wait-state reset no unit without an on-use item reaches), copied with its
  own `assets/` beside it, which `paths::assets_dir` reads first. Same JSONL for every
  Medallion arm; only the Medallion's `items.ron` entry differs:

  | Arm | Medallion entry |
  |---|---|
  | `0` | not equipped (the default loadouts carry no Trinket2) |
  | `H24` / `H48` | `max_health: 24.0` / `48.0`, no `on_use` |
  | `M48` | the break at `price: Some(48.0)`, no stats |
  | `M24` (shipped) | the break at 24, `max_health: 24.0` |

- **Score.** The wearer's team: win 1, draw 0.5, loss 0. Deltas are paired at identical seeds;
  the interval is from the paired differences, so it is set by the matches that flipped.
- Measured on the branch merged with main at `2c63c1ff`.

## Results

| Pair | Delta (pt) | 95% | Flips for / against | McNemar z |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H24 vs 0 | +2.52 | 0.32 | 977 / 402 | 15.5 |
| H48 vs 0 | +5.25 | 0.43 | 1,804 / 607 | 24.4 |
| M48 vs 0 (the break alone) | +2.54 | 0.31 | 872 / 318 | 16.0 |
| M48 vs H48 | -2.72 | 0.49 | 1,237 / 1,874 | 11.4 |
| **M24 vs H48** | **-0.12** | **0.42** | **1,122 / 1,185** | **1.3** |

Health is linear here (24 is half of 48's effect). The break alone (+2.54pt) is worth what 24
health is (+2.52pt), so its declared price is 24, and the shipped item measures level.

**Per wearer class, M24 vs H48** (pt, seed blocks 0 / 1 / 2, pooled with 95%):

|---|---|---|
| **Rogue** | **+4.5 / +4.9 / +5.2** | **+4.8 (1.3)** |
| Shaman | +1.0 / +3.2 / +2.4 | +2.2 (1.5) |
| Druid | +1.4 / +0.8 / -0.6 | +0.5 (1.6) |
| Warrior | +0.5 / +0.6 / -0.3 | +0.3 (1.2) |
| Priest | +0.4 / +0.9 / -1.5 | -0.1 (1.3) |
| Hunter | -1.1 / -0.2 / -0.2 | -0.5 (1.0) |

- **The break is worth most to a Rogue**, in all three blocks: the break alone (M48 vs 0) is
  +7.9pt for a Rogue against +2.5 overall.
- **Mage and Warlock read low in every block**, from opposite ends. The break alone is worth
  nothing measurable to a Mage (M48 vs 0: -0.4pt, +-0.8), and health is worth more to a Warlock
  than to any other class (H48 vs 0: +9.8pt against +5.3 overall; Life Tap spends it). Neither
  moves the price, which is the break's value across the classes; both say a Mage or a Warlock
  is better served by a stat trinket.

## How often it fires

Over 480 traced Medallion matches (a seeded random sample of the sweep's JSONL, `M24` arm), the
wearer was held at all in 188 and **used the Medallion in 180 (38%)**. It waited for a
teammate's dispel 21 times: 16 waits ended with the Medallion kept (the dispel landed, or the
hold ended), 5 broke free after the wait. Per wearer class, uses run from 22% (Hunter) to 54%
(Warrior).

## The arena life it is priced over

`ARENA_LIFE_SECS` is **37.8s**: the mean post-gate life `realised_uptime_against_the_long_run_bound`
(`tests/proc_trinket_probes.rs`) measures on current main, the figure the proc pricing cites.
The talents proposal's 43s was measured before the 0.9 changes. Under it, a 45s cooldown is
still one use and 15s of +40 spell power prices at about 24 (it was 21 over 43s).

## Reproduce

`docs/design/balance/sweeps/2026-10-31-as305-medallion.py` generates the cells, builds each arm
(a binary copy with its own assets and the Medallion entry rewritten), runs it at `--jobs 3`,
and prints the paired analysis and the fire count:

```bash
S=docs/design/balance/sweeps/2026-10-31-as305-medallion.py
cargo build --release
python3 $S gen out
python3 $S arm out 0   --none           --bin target/release/arenasim
python3 $S arm out H24 --health 24      --bin target/release/arenasim
python3 $S arm out H48 --health 48      --bin target/release/arenasim
python3 $S arm out M48 --break 48       --bin target/release/arenasim
python3 $S arm out M24 --break 24 --health 24 --bin target/release/arenasim
python3 $S analyze out M24 H48
python3 $S fires out --bin out/armM24/arenasim --n 480
```
