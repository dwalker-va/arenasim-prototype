How to read it

Rates, lives, speeds, gravity, colour ramps and emission windows come from the parsed M2 files (see the research doc for AS-137). Three things are inferred. Heroic Strike's landing uses a small burst, because its rate tracks literally yield one or two particles. Rings default to facing the camera, because the particle flag that might mean "lie flat" is only community-documented. The casting orb and its fizzle "sputter" are an approximation of casting_orbs.rs, not a copy.

What you sign off is the combination of layer toggles and multipliers for every effect. The constants block collects all of them at once.
