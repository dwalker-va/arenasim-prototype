What the client data says

      
    
        
  • Same skeleton as PW:S. The rim card, both streak cards, all three sequences (birth 700, loop 633, death 1100 ms), every scale key and every texture-weight key are identical to holydivineshield_state_base.m2. Both attach at the feet.

  • Blue fill genericglow64: a soft filled disc on two cards (rim-sized and streak-sized), colour (0.318, 0.396, 1.0), on the rim's weight track. It tints the body blue-white. PW:S is hollow.
    • 
        
  • Streak A shockwave10d is cyan (0.318, 0.91, 1.0) here and yellow on PW:S. Streak B stays gold.
    • 
        
  • Sparkles: two star5a emitters (X-shaped glints), 30/s each, spinning ±3 rad/s, thrown out of a ~1 yd sphere at waist height at 1.67 client yd/s for 0.4 s. Orange to pale to yellow. They start 300 ms into the birth and ramp to zero across the death.
    • 
        
  • Halo fall genericglow2c: a ring sprite dropping from the crown to the feet at 15/s, swelling at the chest. Its enabled track has keys only in the death (on, then off at 300 ms). ambiguous Read per sequence, it falls for the whole shield; read literally, only 4-5 rings fall at the end. Its texture peaks near 8% and its alpha at 0.37, so either way it is near invisible.
    • 
        
  • Caster: kit 417, the same Holy two-hand launch PW:S plays (kit 270), plus a sound.
    • 
        
  • Shipped today: a solid gold egg 1.17 × 1.82 × 1.17 at emissive 3.0, pulsing ±5% every 2.1 s, on and off in one frame. It is 1.2× wider and 1.46× taller than the client rim, and its only difference from the old PW:S sphere was size and brightness.
    • 
        
  • Bench limits: the canvas adds in sRGB with no bloom; Bevy adds in linear HDR and blooms, so the game reads hotter. Cards are drawn in front of the body, which is how the shipped PW:S sorts. The sparkle spawn sphere and the particle scale (read as half-size) are best readings of the emitter record.
