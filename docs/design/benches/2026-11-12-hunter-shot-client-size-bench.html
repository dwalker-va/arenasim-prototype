Hunter Shot Bench

AS-136 · AS-463

The Hunter's shots as the Classic client draws them. Aimed, Arcane and Concussive Shot share one violet shot and one landing. Serpent Sting is the green, dripping variant with a lingering cloud. Auto Shot is the only real arrow. Switch to "Now" to see what ships today.

AS-463, particle size: the client draws an M2 particle at twice its scale key (the key is a radius); this bench was signed off drawing it at the key (half size). "Client size" (default) draws every particle at 2 × key with the size knobs at 1.0; "Signed off" is the bench as signed off. Every other knob is the same in both. The floor is a flat sRGB sand disc, not the calibrated sand through the game's tonemap, so additive glows read a little stronger here than in a match.

Emitter rates, lives, speeds, gravity and colour ramps are from the parsed M2 files. Two things are inferred: the missile core's tint (from its texture, purple_glow.blp), and which emitters lie flat (from particle flags documented by the community). Flight speed is our RON projectile_speed; the client leaves it as an engine constant.
