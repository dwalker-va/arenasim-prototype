UI Design System Bench

AS-251. The proposed tokens and components for every ArenaSim egui screen, drawn in the client face (Rajdhani Bold) on the proposed colour roles. Move a knob on the right and every component re-renders; the Rust block at the bottom is what src/ui/theme.rs would contain. Companion to docs/design/proposals/2026-10-11-ui-design-system.md.

1 · Colour roles

Chrome roles are the encyclopedia's palette (src/states/encyclopedia/mod.rs:87-96), which three screens already copy by hand. Data roles come from results_ui.rs:80-85 and encyclopedia/auras.rs:87-88. HUD and FCT colours are NOT here: they stay in the world/HUD layer (colour budget rule).

2 · Type scale (Rajdhani Bold)

Seven steps replace 23 of the 26 distinct literal sizes in today's screens (9, 10, 11, 11.5, 12, 12.5, 13, 13.5, 14, 14.5, 15, 16, 18, 19, 20, 22, 24, 28, 32, 36, 40, 42, 48). The other three are the 72pt main-menu wordmark (its own WORDMARK token) and the HUD's 96/120pt countdown digits, which stay HUD-local.

4 · Screen header + navigation cluster

ui::widgets::screen_header(ui, title, nav). Back is disabled, never hidden, at the root; the exit button NAMES its destination (the encyclopedia's rule, mod.rs:418-477). Every screen gets this one header, so Back/Exit live in the same place everywhere.

5 · Buttons

Five variants: Primary (one per screen: the forward action), Secondary (default), Ghost (inline/low-weight), Danger (removes something), Icon (square, glyph only). Disabled is a state of each, not a variant. Hover = raised fill + strong line + accent label (the encyclopedia's widget visuals).

8 · HUD layer (stays separate)

Warrior
The in-match HUD keeps its own broadcast rules: labels white, FCT hues reserved (yellow damage, green heal, light-blue absorb), resource colours from one table. It adopts the type scale, spacing and radii, and the shared tooltip, but NOT the menu chrome palette.
