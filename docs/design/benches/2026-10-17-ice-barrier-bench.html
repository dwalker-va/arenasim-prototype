Ice Barrier Bench · AS-358

It is Power Word: Shield's three hollow billboard cards dressed in frost blue and much brighter, plus a ring of icicle clusters on the cards and a falling frost mist. Left: the Frost sphere we ship today. Right: the client model on our rig (×1.229), drawn with procedural stand-ins for the client art. What the client data says

      
    
        
  • The skeleton is PW:S's. The same three quads (rim 3.18 × 4.08, streaks 2.26 × 2.90 client units), the same cylindrical-billboard bone (flags 0x240), the same bone-scale tracks (birth pop, jelly wobble, death swell) and the same three textures, all additive (blend 4).
    • 
        
  • Colour: rim (0.584, 0.82, 1.0), streaks (0.514, 0.741, 1.0) and (0.478, 0.733, 0.992).
    • 
        
  • Much louder held state: the rim sits at 0.12–0.30 (PW:S 0.06–0.15), streak A at 0.6–1.0 (PW:S 0–0.2) and streak B at 0–0.6, alternating every ~100 ms. Ice Barrier is a bright shimmering ring for its whole life. PW:S is a faint one.
    • 
        
  • Icicles (ice3b_b, 497 vertices, on the card): five clusters of 3–5 downward icicles, two at the shoulders and two at the knees, sitting just inside the rim. Drawn twice: a plain pass at 0.6 and a sphere-mapped glint at 0.1 (shader 144). They pop and wobble with the cards.
    • 
        
  • Frost mist (toonsmoke16, an 8 × 8 flipbook): 11.7 puffs/s spawned 0.5 yd above the head, falling 1.67 u/s for 1.8 s inside a 13° cone, growing 0.28 → 0.56 and peaking at alpha 0.39 mid-life. Blue to pale to white. It runs from the cast, with no 200 ms delay.
    • 
        
  • Death, 1100 ms: the swell and rim fade of PW:S; the icicles fade out by 834 ms and the glint by 167 ms; the mist stops spawning at 767 ms and its last puffs fall for another 1.8 s. One ending for break and expiry. ours The shatter is an authored option for a break, off by default.
    • 
        
  • Cast, kit 207: ice_precast_high_hand.m2 on both spell hands for 1 s: a deep-blue glow card (0.76 u) and a ring pulsing every 533 ms, with fine sparks. No Mage spell draws hands today.
    • 
        
  • Shipped today: a solid Frost sphere (0.9 × 1.4 × 0.9, emissive (0.4, 1.0, 2.0)) that appears and vanishes on one frame, for the barrier's 60 s.
    • 
        
  • Bench limits: the canvas adds in sRGB with no bloom, so the game reads hotter. The icicles and mist are procedural stand-ins drawn from the measured positions, sizes and colours. No client art is used. The glint is approximated as a sheen that follows the camera's yaw. With push below 0.5 the cards are drawn behind the body, which is what the depth test gives.
