What the client data says

      
    
        
  • Ground pool: two flat 13.9 yd glow quads (genericglow2_64, genericglow64), additive, orange (0.992, 0.467, 0). Their texture weights are the pulse: quad A swings 0.05 ↔ 0.10 every 100 ms for the whole 30 s (a 5 Hz shimmer); quad B eases 0.10 → 0.05 over the first 15.2 s, then swings 0.05 ↔ 0.10 every 167 ms (3 Hz). The two beat against each other in the second half, so a dying flare sputters harder. Both fall to 0 over the last 0.5 s.
    • 
        
  • The flare itself lies on the ground (0.2 yd up), not in the air: flare.blp sprites, 0.667 yd, 10/s living 0.4 s, colour white-gold → orange → gold. Twinkle at 30 Hz with size jumping 0.4×–1.1×: that is the flicker. Its rate steps 10 → 20/s at 29.0 s and → 5/s at 29.5 s: a last flare-up, then out.
    • 
        
  • Sparks: 25/s at the start, falling linearly to 0 at 29 s, launched into the upper hemisphere at 1.67 yd/s under 1.39 yd/s² gravity, 1 s life, 0.139 → 0 yd, red → orange → pale yellow. The flare spits less as it burns down.
    • 
        
  • Burn-out smoke: 20/s from 29.33 to 29.97 s, 3 s life, 0.11 yd puffs in dark red → dark blue, additive. Near-invisible at our camera: off by default.
    • 
        
  • Not client: the rim marks the 10 yd gameplay radius (the client glow is a soft 7 yd pool), and the halo is a readability glow behind the flare. Both are ours; untick either.
    • 
        
  • Bench limits: canvas adds in sRGB with no bloom; Bevy adds in linear HDR and blooms the emissive, so the client will read somewhat hotter than this.
