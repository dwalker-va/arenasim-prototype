What the client data says

      
    
        
  • Form: three flat cards on one cylindrical-billboard bone (flags 0x240), all unlit additive (blend 4) with no depth write. All three textures are black inside r≈0.45, so the body is never tinted. Only an outline shows.
    • 
        
  • Rim glow genericglow2b1: a ring with a hard inner edge, 3.91 × 5.01 yd on our rig, gold (1.0, 0.91, 0.32). Its inner edge sits 0.98 × 1.25 yd from the centre, which is 0.14 yd above the capsule centre.
    • 
        
  • Streak rings shockwave10d/b: about 17 and 15 soft radial streaks, 2.78 × 3.56 yd, pale gold. They flicker in anti-phase about every 100 ms, which is the shimmer.
    • 
        
  • Birth, 700 ms: nothing for 200 ms. Then a tall, narrow pop (0.52 wide × 1.11 tall) at full strength (weight 1.0) at 300 ms, settling to the loop by 700 ms.
    • 
        
  • Loop, 633 ms: faint. Rim 0.15 → 0.06 → 0.15, streaks 0 ↔ 0.12–0.20, with a slow jelly wobble (width 0.90 ↔ 1.04 against height 1.12 ↔ 1.0).
    • 
        
  • Death, 1100 ms: swells to 1.27 × 1.16 while the rim fades out over 1 s. The client plays this one ending for both break and expiry. ours The break burst is an authored option, off by default.
    • 
        
  • Shipped today: a solid sphere (0.9 × 1.4 × 0.9) at emissive 2.0. It washes the whole body gold for 30 s and appears and vanishes on one frame. The size is close to the client's. The form, the duty cycle and the lifecycle are not.
    • 
        
  • Bench limits: the canvas adds in sRGB with no bloom. Bevy adds in linear HDR and blooms emissive, so the game will read hotter than this. The shipped sphere here is an approximation of its lit colour. With push below 0.5 the card is drawn behind the body, which is what depth testing gives. Our capsule is AlphaMode::Blend with no depth write, so in Bevy the overlap at the legs sorts by entity distance. Use push to settle it.
