Trinket Effects Bench · AS-338 · signed off 2026-10-29

The eight 0.9 trinket effects with a visual, each built from its Classic or TBC client model, over the arena sand the way the client composites it: in linear HDR, then through Bevy 0.16's TonyMcMapface LUT, with the sand calibrated off the 0.8 review screenshot. The scene, LUT and calibration are AS-401's shield bench. The canvas is the centre 960 × 540 of a 1080p game view at 1:1, and playback is real time. Each effect fires on its own cycle. Click a unit, or a tab, to tune it.

Contrast against the sand

effectdefaults ΔL*now ΔL*reads?
Peak over each effect's last cycle of the 95th-percentile |ΔL*| (CIE lightness, 0–100) of its pixels over lit sand, the effect drawn alone. 10 or more reads as clear (AS-401). Defaults are the client's layers at client weights, plus what the engine draws today. The sand sits on the tonemapper's shoulder, so additive light barely moves it: every client layer alone measures under 3.

  • Aegis has no original. The client's own item absorbs (Talisman of Arathor, Defiler's Talisman, Runed Fungalcap) all wear visual 1726, which is kit 847, Power Word: Shield's state. The PW:S rig is the faithful borrow, and it already reads (AS-401).

  • Hand of Justice is not Windfury in the client. Its kit is Sword Specialization's (kit 411: an Attack1H swing and sap_cast_base.m2, Cheap Shot's crescents), plus a large blue floor ripple under both units.

Bench limits

      
    
        
  • Rune, glyph and ring textures are procedural stand-ins for the client BLPs (aurarune_a, aspect_wolf, strength_128, firering1a, shockwavewater1); sizes, timings, colours and weights are the client's.
    • 
        
  • No bloom. Lit layers (the aura band, the dispel ribbon, the stun beads) use AS-401's fitted capsule shading, not Bevy's PBR. Cards draw over bodies; floor decals are depth-tested against them.
    • 
        
  • Diamond Flask's drink pose and tankard, and the cast poses, are not drawn.
